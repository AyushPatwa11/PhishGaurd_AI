Threat Intelligence Center

🚨 Urgency & Fear

Phishers often create a false sense of urgency or fear (e.g., "Your account will be suspended in 24 hours"). This triggers a panic response, making victims less likely to scrutinize the message.

🔗 Domain Spoofing

Attackers use look-alike domains (e.g., paypaI.com instead of paypal.com) or deceptive subdomains (login.paypal.com.security-check.info) to trick users into trusting a malicious link.

👔 Authority Scams

Also known as CEO fraud or Business Email Compromise (BEC). The attacker pretends to be a high-ranking executive requesting an urgent wire transfer or gift cards.

🎁 Scarcity & Greed

Offers that seem too good to be true, like "You've won a $1000 gift card!" These tap into greed and the fear of missing out (FOMO) to steal personal information.
